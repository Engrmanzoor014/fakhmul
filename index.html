Our Story

Pure Nature from the Roof of the World

FAKHMUL was born from a deep love for the pristine mountains of Gilgit Baltistan — a land where nature thrives untouched at altitudes above 2,000 metres.

We work directly with local farmers and foragers to bring you the most authentic dried fruits, wild nuts, and mountain-grown herbs — no middlemen, no additives, just pure goodness.

Mountain Sourced
Every product comes from the high-altitude valleys of Hunza, Skardu, Nagar and Gilgit.
Farmer Direct
We partner with local families, ensuring fair trade and authentic products.
Zero Additives
No preservatives, no chemicals — just naturally dried, naturally good.
